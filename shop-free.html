Free downloads and checks to help you get started. No payment needed.

Wattlewood Free Preview

Free

One sample week of Wattlewood for Year 1-2, 3-4 or 5-6: 20 tasks in each, six themes, difficulty levels and an evidence report. Delivered as an HTML file and a TXT file. Works offline, and Chrome or Edge work best.

Get the free preview (opens in a new tab)

Where to start check

Free

A short, private check for Maths and English, Years 1-7, that suggests a starting level and which year pack to look at. Runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere, and it works offline. Chrome or Edge work best.

Try the Where to start check

Just Left School? Your First Weeks Home

Free

A learning log for the first weeks at home, plus the first official registration steps for your state or territory. One HTML file. It works offline, and you can fill it in on screen or print it. It does not suggest a year level. That is a different tool, the Where to start check.

Open the first weeks kit

State-specific learning plans for all eight states and territories, with PDF export. One HTML file. In Tasmania it is a thinking aid only: the program you submit must be in your own words.
