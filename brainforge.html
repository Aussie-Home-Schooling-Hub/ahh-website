BrainForge

For Years 3–6, ages 8–12. Your child turns research into their own informative writing. You get a record you can show your registering authority.

No student is selected, so work on this page will not be saved. Add a student or choose one from Student profile. BrainForge will not guess which child the work belongs to.

1. Research and facts

Paste the research, then pull out three facts in your own notes.

The source is hidden so you write from your three facts.

Three facts

Use a date, a figure, or a specialised word. All three are needed.

Now write using your three facts.

2. Write it in your own words

Add a main title, subheaders, and paragraphs in your own words.

Australian Curriculum text structures require a clear title so the reader immediately knows the overarching topic.

Subheaders act as conceptual signposts, grouping related facts together so your informative text flows logically.

Pasting is switched off here. Type it in your own words and it will stick in your memory.
