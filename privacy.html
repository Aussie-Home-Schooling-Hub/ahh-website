Privacy policy

How Aussie Homeschooling Hub collects, uses and looks after your personal information, in plain English.

Who we are

Aussie Homeschooling Hub is run by Peter Kroon, trading as Aussie Homeschooling Hub (ABN 37 791 164 057), based in Nirimba, Queensland. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Aussie Homeschooling Hub.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

We only collect what we need. That includes:

  • Giveaway entries: your name, email address, state or territory, the year level you choose, your answer to the entry question, and your confirmation that you're 18 or over and accept the terms.
  • Email sign-ups: your email address, and your name if you give it, when you join one of our email lists or download a free product that says it adds you to a list.
  • Payhip orders: when you buy or download from our Payhip store, Payhip handles the order and payment. We see your name, email address and what you ordered. We never see your full card details.
  • Contact and waitlist forms on this website: your name, email address and message, or your email address if you join a waitlist.

We collect this information directly from you, when you fill in a form, sign up, enter a giveaway, place an order or email us.

Our Wattlewood apps and other downloads keep your child's information on your own device. It isn't sent to us unless you choose to send it.

Why we collect it

  • To run giveaways: check and judge entries, contact winners and deliver prizes.
  • To deliver what you buy or download, and help if something goes wrong.
  • To reply to your messages and let you know when something you joined a waitlist for is ready.
  • To send our emails, but only if you've chosen to receive them.

We don't use your information for anything else without asking you first, unless the law requires or allows it.

Marketing emails

We only send marketing emails to people who opt in. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time. You can also email us and we'll take you off the list.

Entering a giveaway doesn't add you to our email list. You only join it if you tick the separate, optional box on the entry form.

Giveaway winners

If you win a giveaway, we may announce your first name and state on our Facebook and Instagram pages, as set out in that giveaway's terms on our giveaways page. We don't publish any other details.

We never sell your information

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share it with the services below, so they can do their job for us, or where the law requires it.

Services we use

  • Google Forms (Google) for giveaway entries.
  • Payhip (Payhip Ltd, United Kingdom) for our online store, orders, payments and downloads. Payhip's own privacy policy also applies when you use the store: payhip.com/privacy (opens in a new tab).
  • EmailOctopus (Three Hearts Digital Ltd, United Kingdom) to store our email lists and send our emails.
  • Netlify to host this website and receive our contact and waitlist forms.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta) to share news and announce giveaway winners by first name and state.

Information stored outside Australia

These services store information overseas. That means your information is likely to be held outside Australia, including in:

  • the European Union (EmailOctopus stores lists in Ireland)
  • the United Kingdom (Payhip and EmailOctopus)
  • the United States and other countries where Google, Netlify and Meta keep their data centres.

We choose established services that publish their own privacy and security practices.

Our website

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Like most websites, our host keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure. Our fonts load from Google Fonts. Our Payhip store uses its own cookies, explained in Payhip's privacy policy.

How we look after it

We keep your information in password-protected accounts and only use it for the reasons above. We keep giveaway entries only as long as we need them to run the giveaway, contact winners and keep basic records.

You don't have to tell us who you are to browse this website. To enter a giveaway, order or join a list, we need your contact details.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it or delete it. Email us at hello@aussiehomeschoolinghub.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days. There's no charge.

Questions or complaints

If you have a question or you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, email us at hello@aussiehomeschoolinghub.com.au. We'll look into it and reply within 30 days.

If you're not happy with our reply, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the way we handle personal information changes.
