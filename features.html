Every Aussie Homeschooling Hub tool, and how they fit together

Here's everything I make for Australian home educating families on one page: what each tool does, which year levels it covers, and how they work together. Everything runs offline, with no account or login, and your family's records stay on your own device.

The quick version

Year levels

Wattlewood year packs for Year 1-2, Year 3-4, Year 5-6, Year 7 and Year 8. Each pack is 40 weeks and 800 tasks.

Subjects

Wattlewood covers Maths, English, Science and Geography. The weekly PDF curriculum covers English, Maths, Science and HASS.

Dated evidence as your child works, then evidence reports, term reports and certificates you can print or save as a PDF.

The Wattlewood family

Four tools that work together. Start with Wattlewood or Sapling, then add the others if you want them.

Wattlewood: the curriculum app

A daily trail of Australian Curriculum tasks in Maths, English, Science and Geography, for Years 1 to 8. Every task has Support, Core and Extension levels, and you decide when your child's level changes. It supports more than one child.

  • Year packs for Year 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7 and 8, each 40 weeks and 800 tasks
  • Dated evidence saved as your child works
  • Parent Corner: evidence reports, term reports, certificates, learning hours and a weekly debrief
  • Print worksheets for a week or a single day, with answers on a parent page
  • Save Backup File to keep a copy of your data

See example evidence entries

Wattlewood Sapling: the unschooling logbook

A simple, private logbook and reporting tool for unschooling and natural-learning families. Log what happened in your own words, with a photo and tags. Sapling doesn't run year packs.

  • Free-form entries for one child
  • Reports set out for your state or territory, ready to print or save as a PDF
  • Archive a term and start fresh
  • Back up and restore your data

Wattlewood Hollow: interactive modules

The Hollow launcher opens single-file interactive modules. When your child finishes a module, it saves evidence back to Wattlewood. Modules are filed under their learning area in your reports.

  • Junior (free): Build-A-Beast, The Great Emu War, Min Min Lights, The Great Aussie Poo Detective and The Reverse Op Shop
  • Senior: Africa Field Atlas, Veterinary Science Clinic, Australian History Stories, Emergency Comms Centre, and Service and STEM

The Hollow bundle comes with Build-A-Beast and Africa Field Atlas.

Wattlewood Field Notes: the excursion logger

A page on your phone for logging an excursion while you're out: the place, the date, what you did, the focus area and the year levels. Add it to your home screen and it works offline.

Each excursion gets a QR code and a sync code. Scan the QR code or paste the code into Wattlewood or Sapling, and the excursion is added to your records.

How it fits together

  1. Try it free

    The free preview is one sample week of Wattlewood for Year 1-2, 3-4 or 5-6, with an evidence report. The free Where to start check suggests a starting level for Maths and English.

  2. Daily work goes in one place

    Your child works through Wattlewood (or you log learning in Sapling). Hollow modules and Field Notes excursions add to the same records.

  3. Reports when you need them

    Print or save evidence reports, term reports and certificates from Parent Corner. Save a backup file regularly so your records are safe.

Work photos stay on your device and aren't included in the backup file, so keep your original photos and save your Evidence Report as a PDF each term. Your registering authority decides about your registration; these tools help you record and present the learning.
