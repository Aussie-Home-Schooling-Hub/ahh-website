Freebie Sunday: Wattlewood Giveaway — Terms and Conditions

1. Promoter

The promoter is Peter Kroon, trading as Aussie Homeschooling Hub, ABN 37 791 164 057, Nirimba QLD ("the Promoter").

2. About this giveaway

Freebie Sunday is a giveaway where winners are chosen by judging. It is a game of skill, not a lottery or game of chance. There is no random draw.

3. Who can enter

3.1 Entry is open only to Australian residents aged 18 or over.

3.2 Entry is not open to the Promoter or its officers, employees or contractors, or to their immediate family.

3.3 One entry per person. If someone enters more than once, only their first valid entry counts.

4. Entry period

Entries open at 9:00am AEST (Queensland time) on Monday 16 November 2026, or later if these terms are published later. Entries close at 5:00pm AEST on Friday 20 November 2026. Entries received after the close are not accepted.

5. How to enter

5.1 Complete the official entry form at https://docs.google.com/forms/d/13RdBrGJRYqJJeEhpJ6k2YQCbUtkfUhE90kAROJICS6c/viewform (opens in a new tab) during the entry period.

5.2 The form asks for:

  • your name and email address
  • your state or territory
  • the year level you would choose
  • confirmation that you are 18 or over and accept these terms
  • a written answer of 25 words or less to this question: "What is one thing that would most help your family with planning or evidence this year, and why?"

5.3 Entry is free. No purchase is needed.

5.4 You don't need to like, comment on, share or tag anyone in a post, or post on a personal timeline, to enter. Doing any of these will not earn an extra entry.

5.5 An entry must be complete and the entrant's own work. Entries that are incomplete or go over the 25-word limit may be treated as invalid.

6. Prizes

6.1 There are two (2) prizes. Each prize is one copy of the Wattlewood app on Payhip (https://payhip.com/b/BmOoR (opens in a new tab)). It includes one year level of the winner's choice (Year 1-2, Year 3-4, Year 5-6 or Year 7), as offered on the Payhip product page at the time of award.

6.2 Extra year packs, Wattlewood Hollow modules, Wattlewood Workshop and other add-ons are not included.

6.3 Prizes are not redeemable for cash.

7. Judging

7.1 On Saturday 21 November 2026, Peter Kroon will judge all valid entries on:

  • how clear the answer is
  • how genuine the home-education need is
  • how thoughtfully the entrant would use a year-level Wattlewood setup

7.2 The two highest-judged entries win. Chance plays no part.

7.3 The Promoter keeps notes of the judging.

8. Telling the winners

8.1 Winners will be emailed on Saturday 21 November 2026, at the email address given on the entry form.

8.2 Winners may be announced by first name and state on the Aussie Homeschooling Hub Facebook and Instagram pages on Sunday 22 November 2026.

8.3 If a winner doesn't claim their prize within 7 days of the notification email, the Promoter may award the prize to the next-best judged entry.

9. Prize delivery

Within 7 days of a winner's claim, the Promoter will email them a single-use 100% Payhip coupon for the Wattlewood app and the product link. The winner chooses their year level at checkout.

10.3 A winner's first name and state may be published as set out in clause 8.2.

10.4 See our privacy policy: https://aussiehomeschoolinghub.com.au/privacy
