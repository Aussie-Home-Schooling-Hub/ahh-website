Two jobs, one tool

Most families need help with the same two things: knowing what to do today, and having something to show afterwards.

Something real to do today

Your child gets a daily trail of Australian Curriculum tasks in Maths, English, Science and Geography. Every task has Support, Core and Extension levels. Your child's level never changes by itself: the app can suggest a change, and you decide.

On the days you have nothing left to invent, the work is already there.

Learning that becomes evidence

As your child works, Wattlewood keeps dated evidence. Parent Corner has evidence reports, term reports, certificates and learning hours, so you aren't rebuilding the year from memory when a report is due.
